# Weather radar in the public build

On **Clock & Favourites**, select **Radar** beside the forecast. The head unit opens [Windy weather radar](https://www.windy.com/-Weather-radar-radar) in its browser. Use the site’s search for your location and Android’s Back button to return.

The APK sends the browser to a fixed HTTPS page. The URL carries no coordinates, settings or vehicle readings. The browser and provider handle map requests. AVC Manager’s forecast has its own location and consent settings under **Controls → Weather**, with Open-Meteo credits in the weather panel. [Windy privacy policy](https://account.windy.com/agreements/windy-privacy-policy)
