Toyota pressure2104 uses U16(J,K)/100 minus327.68kPa. This conflicts with the
signed SAE017A interpretation seen on this ECU; the Toyota candidate remains
experimental. The implausible signed comparison is excluded from this profile.
The standard definition remains available to other vehicle profiles.

Oil temperature,018B regeneration fields, diagnostic service-regeneration status,
regeneration observations and unmapped regeneration counters are excluded from
this Toyota profile at the owner's request. No015C,2151 or018B probe is sent.
