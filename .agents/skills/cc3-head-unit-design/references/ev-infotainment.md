# Production EV infotainment benchmark

This is a model-level desk review of major global groups and selected EV specialists, with extra coverage of models sold in Australia. It uses manufacturer manuals, product imagery and specifications. Equipment and software vary by market and trim. Interaction timing, glare and reach require in-car testing.

## Most useful for CC3

| Manufacturer and model | Interface pattern | CC3 interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Polestar 4** | Landscape map with editable widgets, a movable divider, and permanent Home and app library access. [Owner's manual](https://www.polestar.com/us/manual/polestar-4/2025/article/47d2c97fd33effd3c0a8cc3718c999b7-655ba57eb6bd2eedc0a8b0971b5b532a-8664b2fa77a7e089c0a8296870d1a409/) · [Screen imagery](https://www.polestar.com/en-ae/polestar-4/infotainment/) | Best starting point for a landscape Home layout. | **BMW iX3** | QuickSelect puts vertical widgets near the driver and keeps Home and common destinations at the lower edge. [BMW iX3 press kit](https://newsroom.bmwgroup.com/americas/the-all-new-bmw-ix3) | The current right-side rail suits the Fortuner. Keep it stable and check reach from the seat. Put navigation and media above configuration in the shortcut order. |

[ID.7 interior](https://www.volkswagen-newsroom.com/en/the-new-id7-pro-s-and-id7-tourer-pro-s-international-media-drive-18617/interior-18626) · [ID. The Fortuner already has physical cabin controls. One in-app dock leaves more height for content. |
| **Tesla Model 3** | Map-led screen, expandable media, persistent controls and editable My Apps favourites. [Australian owner's manual](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/model3/en_au/GUID-518C51C1-E9AC-4A68-AE12-07F4FF8C881E.html) | Make favourites quick to reach and provide a visible edit entry. [Owner's manual, Display bars](https://www.volvocars.com/static/support-content/pdfs/uk_en-GB_ex30_2026_UM_95a84d18368d7f4f065cb64a17a8ef6f.pdf) | Reserve a predictable place for conditional status or playback controls. | **Peugeot E-3008** | Programmable i-Toggles jump directly to chosen functions, including a radio station or navigation destination. [Peugeot press kit](https://www.media.stellantis.com/em-en/peugeot/press/new-peugeot-e-3008-next-level-electric-fastback-suv) | Allow supported actions and destinations in favourites alongside app launches. ## How to use these sources

- Start with Google for glanceability, reach, colour, and automotive sizing. Treat Android for Cars values as design benchmarks for this aftermarket unit. [Google Design for Driving](https://developers.google.com/cars/design)
- Use Femto for visual examples and the build, navigate, capture and inspect loop. Adapt its emulator profile to the CC3. Its own launcher requires Android 13 or later. [Femto screenshots](https://seijikohara.github.io/femto-car-launcher/catalog/) · [Femto verification skill](https://github.com/seijikohara/femto-car-launcher/blob/main/.claude/skills/verify-on-emulator/SKILL.md)
- Borrow explicit Back and system-bar awareness from Google's parked-app skill. Apply those ideas using the CC3's existing Android Views and Android 10 APIs. [Google's parked-app skill](https://github.com/android/skills/blob/main/cars/android-for-cars-parked-apps/SKILL.md)
- For visual exploration, borrow MockFlow's high-contrast dashboard framing and Material 3's consistency across type, colour and component states. The CC3 skill works with the project's own screenshot and native View workflow. [MockFlow Automotive HMI](https://mockflow.com/skills/automotive-hmi-infotainment) · [Android design agent skill](https://github.com/charliesbot/android-design)
