# Landscape visual acceptance

**Start with the usable rectangle.** Record pixel resolution, density, font scale, system-bar insets and rail side. The tested Fortuner CC3 uses Android 10, 1280 × 720, 160 dpi, 1.5× text and a 60 px top bar. That leaves 1280 × 660 px for the app before its own navigation. A second model needs its own measurements. Reserve room for the vendor's clock, voltage and connectivity indicators.

**Compose around the task.** On Home, let the clock, date and timezone measure together, including the analogue dial. Reserve equal caption space for wrapped and single-line shortcut names. Allocate the map or graph its own usable area. Keep All apps and Manager reachable from each layout, including layouts with few favourites. Lists such as the media library or all sensors may scroll inside their browsing area. Keep their actions visible. Treat a no-scroll Home as a layout constraint. Shrinking the type to force everything into view obscures the original problem.

**Compare with the factory UI when appearance is in scope.** Look at the actual model's interface or the manufacturer's identifiable screen image. Separate screen colours from advertising, wallpaper and album art. For example, TEYES shows indigo panels with cyan and violet on the CC3 and a charcoal map-and-media dock on the CC4. Use the existing palette for surfaces, text, selection, focus, system bars and native widgets. Keep operational status distinguishable with a word, indicator or check mark. In AVC Manager, Midnight Blue and Obsidian are factory-inspired interpretations. Custom colour selection and wallpaper are separate preferences. **Verify what the driver sees.** Capture a native PNG and the corresponding accessibility tree after the layout settles. Inspect at original size. The tree can show that a label or touch target is clipped. It cannot prove that the fill, icon and first text baseline align. Compare adjacent painted edges, the gap beneath a clock face, and the first lines of long and short labels. Exercise the control and return. Select locally relevant cases such as an empty library, missing DSP, stale readings, or more than one row of shortcuts. Report simulated data as simulated.

For a flat-fill alignment question, the bundled [painted-edge checker](../scripts/painted_edges.py) accepts two or more interior pixel coordinates on the original screenshot. Sample near a straight side, clear of text, icons, strokes and round corners. Read the reported top and bottom for each control. A photograph, gradient, shadow, or textured background needs direct visual inspection. For the historical CC3 Wireless comparison, these points sit inside Wi-Fi settings and Access points.

```sh
python3 scripts/painted_edges.py wireless.png --at 50,408 --at 592,408
```

The command is relative to the skill directory and uses Pillow. The tolerance is the permitted difference between painted edge positions in pixels. Evaluate text contrast separately using foreground and actual rendered surface colours. On driving surfaces, 4.5:1 text contrast and a 76 dp target are useful automotive design benchmarks. Use the applicable platform guidance for parked settings and OEM components.
